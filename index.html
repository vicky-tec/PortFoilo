01. About Me

Hi, I’m Vicky Raj — a Data Analyst with a strong foundation in frontend web development and a growing focus on AI-driven solutions and automation. I’m currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Data Analytics at IIT Patna, where I continuously sharpen my analytical and problem-solving skills.

I enjoy working with data from raw to ready — cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing it to uncover insights that support clear, data-backed decision-making. Using tools like Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI, I love exploring patterns, identifying trends, and translating complex datasets into simple, meaningful stories.

I see AI not as a shortcut, but as a powerful assistant — helping me think better, build faster, and solve smarter.
